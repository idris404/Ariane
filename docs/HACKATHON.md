# AI for Health 2026

## Event

Ariane was built during the **AI for Health** hackathon, held in Paris from **November 13 to 15, 2026**, at Metafore Boétie Saint-Augustin.

For 50 hours, the event brought together health, data, artificial intelligence, development, design, and entrepreneurship profiles to turn field needs into prototypes.

Event page: [Hackathon Adopt AI for Health](https://hackathons-artefactadoptai.agorize.com/fr/challenges/hackathon-adopt-ai-for-health?lang=en).

## Award

🏆 **Best Patient Solution Award**

The award recognizes Ariane's proposal: prepare review work, keep records requiring special attention visible, and leave the final decision in professional hands.

## Built in 50 hours

The presented prototype includes:

- a coherent identity and product workflow;
- a landing page and demonstration video;
- a frontend with multiple operational screens;
- a FastAPI API and local PostgreSQL database;
- a deterministic synthetic data pipeline;
- validation and consultation queues;
- backend, data, and web workflow tests.

## Presented result

The 1,000-profile synthetic scenario places 714 records in the queue prepared for validation and 286 records in the deeper-review queue. This distribution describes only the prototype presented to the jury.
